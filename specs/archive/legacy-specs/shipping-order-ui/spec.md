# Shipping order UI

## Business outcome

Give a WMS operator a first interface for finding a shipping order, reviewing its
contents, selecting its shipping location, and starting picking.

## Scope

- Paged and filtered shipping-order list.
- Details with header information and shipping items.
- Zone-dependent selection of a shipping location limited to the order warehouse.
- Persist the selected location, then set a prepared order ready for picking.

## Non-goals

- A picking work page or editing picking movements in the UI.
- Ready-for-shipment and shipping actions in the UI.
